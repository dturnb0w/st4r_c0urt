# Spices

Ongoing project. Most of what people call "secret" is just ratio, but a few of these
are genuinely strange and I am keeping notes.

| Spice | Where from | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Cardamom | the good store two towns over | The whole trick. Do not substitute |
| Saffron | mail order, expensive, suspicious | Stains everything. Possibly not legal |
| Star anise | Chinatown, Indianapolis | Looks magical. Tastes like liquorice betrayal |
| Nutmeg | anywhere | A trap. See secret-spice.md |
| Sumac | nowhere near here | Never found any. Continuing the search |
